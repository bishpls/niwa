niwa

A world built entirely in code, explored in your browser. Open it (it opens by itself in a few seconds).

It needs a desktop browser with WebGL2 (Chrome or Edge is best) and a machine with plenty of memory: 16 GB is comfortable. It builds the whole world on your machine as it loads, so the first load takes a while (up to two minutes; a progress bar shows where it is).
Drag to orbit, right-drag or shift-drag to pan, scroll to zoom, WASD to fly, 1-9 for viewpoints; the panel on the right holds the time of day, the reel (with sound) and every setting.
